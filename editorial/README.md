# Editorial

Skills for long-form writing: articles, blog posts, memos, and essays. Covers drafting doctrine, editing, scoring, and the final AI-tell scrub.

## Ledger

| Skill | What it does | Use when | Files |
|---|---|---|---|
| [business-writing-manifesto](business-writing-manifesto/SKILL.md) | Seven principles and three laws of tightness, organized as a before/while/before-ship operating system | Deciding if a piece is worth shipping, and cutting it down to what teaches or enables action | `SKILL.md` |
| [claudisms](claudisms/SKILL.md) | A living banlist of AI-writing tells (words, structural tics, register tics, spoken-word tells) with replacements | **Last pass on every draft, in every department.** Also useful as a check while drafting | `SKILL.md` |
| [rohan-writing-doctrine](rohan-writing-doctrine/SKILL.md) | How Rohan writes, edits, and judges, plus his Claude-tell catalog and a 12-item pre-flight checklist | ⚠️ **Opt-in only.** Use it only when someone explicitly asks for the Rohan Doctrine. A Rohan byline alone is not enough | `SKILL.md` |
| [tech-blog-analysis](tech-blog-analysis/SKILL.md) | Scores a technical article for CEO and CTO readers and returns a report with an Executive Readiness Score (1-10) | Critiquing or scoring a draft for executive technical buyers. Not for drafting | `SKILL.md` |

## Living files

- **claudisms** grows each time a new tell is flagged. Add the entry to its list.

## Recommended order for an article

1. **tech-blog-analysis**: check structure and expertise level (for executive technical readers).
2. **business-writing-manifesto**: apply the value test to every paragraph.
3. [**hopkins-copywriting**](../copywriting/hopkins-copywriting/SKILL.md): write the title, hook, and any call to action.
4. **rohan-writing-doctrine**: apply his voice, *only when explicitly requested*.
5. **claudisms**: do the final pass.

## When rules conflict

These skills were written for different audiences. Resolve conflicts by asking who is speaking, and to whom.

| Conflict | Under Rohan's byline | Company blog or sales copy |
|---|---|---|
| Paragraph length | 1–3 sentences (Rohan) | 50–150 words (tech-blog-analysis) |
| Numbers | Lived numbers in prose, research numbers as screenshots | Specifics in the sentence (Hopkins) |
| Openings | Hot, rambling open | No story-mode openings |
| Repetition | Say it once, and repeat the same word (never a synonym) for intensity | Say it again, in different words (Hopkins) |

Claudisms applies everywhere, whatever the voice. Hopkins's rule against invented statistics also always applies.
