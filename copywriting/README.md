# Copywriting

Persuasive copy: ads, landing pages, headlines, hooks, social posts, sales pages, and scripts.

## Ledger

| Skill | What it does | Use when | Files |
|---|---|---|---|
| [hormozi-writing](hormozi-writing/SKILL.md) | Writes or rewrites copy by combining Hormozi-style persuasion with Mannan's voice rules (no em-dashes, no AI-sounding phrases, one idea per line). Includes a de-AI pass and adapters for each format | Copy **in Mannan's voice**: LinkedIn posts, white papers, landing pages, ads, video scripts, or "make this sound human / punch this up" | `SKILL.md` |
| [hopkins-copywriting](hopkins-copywriting/SKILL.md) | Claude Hopkins' scientific-advertising method: a 6-step workflow, headline patterns, a before/after gallery, and a 10-point self-check | Headlines, titles, hooks, ads, landing pages, sales emails, and product copy. Also critiquing or scoring existing copy | `SKILL.md` |

## Which one?

- **Mannan's voice** → hormozi-writing.
- **Headlines and calls to action, or brand-neutral persuasive copy** → hopkins-copywriting. It also covers the title, hook, and CTA step in the [editorial](../editorial/README.md) article pipeline.
- **Cold emails for Loom videos** → [`sales/loom-email-copy`](../sales/loom-email-copy/SKILL.md).
- **YouTube titles and descriptions** → [`youtube/`](../youtube/README.md).
- **Words inside a graphic** → [`graphic-design/graphic-copy`](../graphic-design/graphic-copy/SKILL.md).
- After any of these, do a final pass with [`editorial/claudisms`](../editorial/claudisms/SKILL.md).
