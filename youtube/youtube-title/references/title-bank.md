# Ionio YouTube Title Bank (Annotated)

Every published title to date, grouped by type and archetype. Use this as the pattern library: when titling a new video, find the closest published example and match its shape, then check you're not near-duplicating an existing title.

## Regular videos

### Build / teardown
- How We Build AI Features that Open New Revenue Streams
- How We Built a Self-Hosted RAG Framework for Enterprises
- How Our Team Built A Shadcn Alternative For Flutter Ecosystem
- How To Build a Local RAG System for Any YouTube Podcast Channel | No API costs!
- We Built a Self-Learning RAG That Doesn't Require Fine Tuning
- We Built a RAG that learns by itself  *(thumbnail variant of the self-learning RAG video)*
- How we created AI ads that IMPROVE themselves

Pattern: "How We Built / We Built / How To Build" + the artifact + who it's for or what's surprising about it. The twist often sits in a relative clause ("That Doesn't Require Fine Tuning", "that Open New Revenue Streams").

### Contrarian / prediction
- Why GEO Will Replace SEO For E-Commerce | Agentic Commerce #1
- Why Dashboards Are Dying (and What's Replacing Them) | Prescriptive Intelligence
- How Our Prediction for Agentic Commerce Failed... But Not Really | Agentic Commerce #3

Pattern: a claim that pushes against the obvious. `...` carries the cliffhanger; `(parenthetical)` teases the payoff.

### Opinion / stakes story
- Why I Said No to a $30K AI Automation Deal
- How Lovable and V0 made us lose $7K UI/UX deals
- 3 AI Industry Lies That Drive Me Crazy

Pattern: a real decision or cost, anchored by a dollar figure or a named tool. First person, personal stakes.

### Explainer / guide
- What is Ionio Lighthouse?
- How to master personalization like Amazon | Mid-Market ecommerce brand's personalization guide
- How To Create Best Embedding Model for Retail
- How Much Does It Cost to Run a RAG System in 2025?
- Learn Ecommerce Personalization like Amazon  *(thumbnail variant)*
- Want to become an AI developer in 2025? Watch THIS GUIDE first!
- 3 Tips for Hosting Models in Production
- Comprehensive guide to Becoming a Developer (In 2025)  *(thumbnail variant)*

Pattern: teaches a concept end to end. Often anchored by a named comparison (Amazon), a domain (Retail), or a year (2025).

### Problem / critique
- The Fundamental Problem of the GEO Hype | Agentic Commerce #4
- This is the BIGGEST mistake businesses make while adopting AI
- The Secret Email Strategy Top 5% of Brands Are Using

Pattern: name the flaw in a popular thing, or the secret behind the winners. "The [Adjective] [Problem/Strategy]" framing. One CAPS word for punch (BIGGEST).

## Testimonial videos

Structure: `{Name} | {Role} of {Company} | {partner-framed phrase}`

The frame is **AI transformation partner**, never vendor. Some early titles below carry vendor-leaning phrasing ("experience working with Ionio") that the channel is moving away from — they're here for the structural template, not as a phrasing model.

- Thomas | Founder & CEO of Hypemail & Patato | Shares The Ionio Experience
- Greg Warner CEO & Founder of Revvy Speaks about his experience with working with Ionio  *(drops pipes and leans vendor — evolve away from both)*
- Ryan Samii | Founder & CEO of Standard Draft | Shares His Experience Working with Ionio
- Reid Chong | Founder & CEO of Dex | Speaks on His Experience of Working with Ionio
- Shaun Brown | CEO of Orbit Metrics | Speaks About His Experience With Ionio
- Michael Guimarin | Co-Founder of WordOut | Speaks up about working with Ionio
- Eric Bort | Founder & CEO of Clearly Trained | Shares His Views on Ionio
- Eanna Mac Gearlit | Founder & CEO of GrowthLab | The Ionio Experience

Prefer the pipe-delimited form. For the phrase, reach for partner / transformation language (On Building with Ionio, On Transforming {Company} with Ionio, The Ionio Partnership) over "experience working with".

### Thumbnail pull-quotes (live on the thumbnail, not the title)

**Model quotes — partner / transformation frame (aim here):**
- "They Felt Like Real Partners Around the Table" (Standard Draft)
- "Ionio Helped Us Make the Idea Real" (Clearly Trained)
- "They Understood Both the Technical and the Business Side" (GrowthLab)
- "They Didn't Get Rattled When Things Went Wrong" (WordOut)
- "They Restored My Faith in Great Code" (Orbit Metrics)

**Evolve away from — vendor / service-review frame:**
- "They Never Miss a Deadline!" (Hypemail) — reliability-as-service; reframe as trust under pressure
- "We've had the opportunity of working with best full stack software engineering company in the biz." (Revvy) — "engineering company" + superlative reads like a vendor rating
- "A Team We'd Recommend Without Hesitation" (Dex) — referral language; soft vendor tell

Pattern for new quotes: first-person, 3-8 words, the single strongest *partnership* moment, condensed. Pull it from the transcript; don't invent it.
